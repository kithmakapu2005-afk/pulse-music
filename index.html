01Features

Everything your music needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Five things, done properly, for the music already on your phone.

Mobile data off · still playing

Offline music playback

Songs play straight from your phone's storage: on a plane, underground, or with mobile data switched off. Playback carries on in the background, with controls on the lock screen and over Bluetooth.

  • No internet permission
  • Background playback
  • Lock-screen controls

Personal music library

Your songs, organised into albums and artists. Search songs, artists and albums, and pick up again from Recently Played.

Music Neon Horizon Glass City.flac

Music folder import

Pick a folder and Pulse brings in every song inside it, subfolders too. You can also pick single files or scan the whole phone. Removing a folder never deletes your files.

  3. 3

Playlists

Create and name playlists for every mood, rename them whenever you like, and line up songs in the Up Next queue. Deleting a playlist keeps your songs in the library.

Favourites

Tap the heart on any song. Your favourites get their own tab and a place on the Home screen, ready whenever you are.

Also inside

  • Skip silence
  • Playback speed 0.75×–2×
  • Dark, light & system themes

  • Turntable Now Playing screen

03About

Built for the music you own.

Pulse Music is a player for the songs already on your phone, made to be fast, good-looking and private.

There's nothing to sign up for and no catalogue to rent. You bring the files and Pulse turns them into a library you'll enjoy opening, with a spinning turntable, glass panels and a neon glow that follows the beat.

  • Private by design

    Pulse doesn't request Android's Internet permission, and your library lives only on your phone.

  • Light on your phone

    The whole app is a 3.8 MB download.

  • Made for Android

    Written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, and playing through Media3 ExoPlayer.
